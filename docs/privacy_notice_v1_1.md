# Privacy notice: what is new in version 1.1

The frontend must show this text with the privacy notice, at Create account and when
`GET /auth/me` returns `consent_required: true`. The rest of the notice stays as in version 1.0.

## Contact details and Sign in with Google

We now also ask for these details. All of them are **optional**.

- **Contact number:** one mobile number.
- **Home address:** province, city or municipality and barangay, plus an optional street line.
- **Sign in with Google:** Google confirms your email address. We keep your Google account ID and
  your email address.

**Why we ask for them:** [TO BE FILLED IN BY THE GROUP]

These details are not used by the risk models and do not appear in your reports. They are deleted
when you delete your account.
